Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who this covers

Flight Agent is a personal automation used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to any other user and collects nothing from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, the application requests two Google permissions: read only access to the owner's Google Calendar, and permission to send email from the owner's own Gmail account.

What it does with that access

It reads upcoming calendar events to identify flights, checks the public status of those flights with a third party aviation data provider, and sends the owner an email when a flight departs.

What it stores

Only a flight number and departure date for each flight already notified, so the same flight is not reported twice. No calendar content, no message content, and no personal data beyond that is retained.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Flight numbers and dates are sent to an aviation data provider solely to look up flight status.

Retention and removal

Stored records are deleted automatically thirty days after the flight date. The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the owner of this repository.
